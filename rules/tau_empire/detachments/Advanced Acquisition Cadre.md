# Advanced Acquisition Cadre

**T'au Empire** - 1 DP detachment - Force Disposition: Reconnaissance

Source: <https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/t-au-empire/>

## Detachment rule

### Expert Fieldcraft

In your Shooting phase, when a friendly PATHFINDER TEAM/STEALTH BATTLESUITS unit is **selected to shoot**, those ranged attacks do not prevent your unit from being **hidden**.

## Enhancements

### Negation Emitters - 15 pts

STEALTH BATTLESUITS unit only. This unit has -3" **detection range**.

### Unmasking Suite - 15 pts

GHOSTKEEL BATTLESUIT/PATHFINDER TEAM/STEALTH BATTLESUITS unit only. When this unit is **selected to shoot**, you can select one enemy unit within 24" of this unit. That enemy unit has +9" **detection range** until this unit has shot.

## Stratagems

### MARKER BEACON - 1CP

*Advanced Acquisition Cadre Stratagem*

**WHEN:** End of your Movement phase.

**TARGET:** One friendly PATHFINDER TEAM/STEALTH BATTLESUITS unit.

**EFFECT:** Select one **objective** your unit is controlling. That **objective** is **secured**.

### MICRODRONE SUPPORT - 1CP

*Advanced Acquisition Cadre Stratagem*

**WHEN:** Your Shooting phase, when a friendly PATHFINDER TEAM/ STEALTH BATTLESUITS unit **starts an action**.

**TARGET:** That PATHFINDER TEAM/STEALTH BATTLESUITS unit.

**EFFECT:** That **action** does not prevent your unit from being **eligible to shoot**.

### AUTOREACTIVE CAMOUFLAGE - 1CP

*Advanced Acquisition Cadre Stratagem*

**WHEN:** Your opponent’s Shooting phase, when an enemy unit targets a friendly PATHFINDER TEAM/ STEALTH BATTLESUITS unit, if that friendly unit is **hidden**.

**TARGET:** That PATHFINDER TEAM/STEALTH BATTLESUITS unit.

**EFFECT:** Your unit has +1 **Sv**.
