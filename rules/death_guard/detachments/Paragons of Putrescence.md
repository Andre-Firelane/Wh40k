# Paragons of Putrescence

**Death Guard** - 1 DP detachment - Force Disposition: Priority Assets

Source: <https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/death-guard/>

## Detachment rule

### Hypervirulent Strains

Friendly DEATH GUARD CHARACTER units have +3" to their **Contagion Range** (to a maximum of 12").

## Enhancements

### Rejuvenating Swarm - 20 pts

DEATH GUARD INFANTRY model only (excluding TERMINATOR models). Attacks that target this unit with a **S** greater than this unit’s **T** have -1 to **wound rolls**.

### Host of the Hybridised Pox - 40 pts

DEATH GUARD INFANTRY model only. (Once per battle, per army) In your Command phase, you can use this ability. If you do, select one of the Plague listed in **Nurgle’s Gift**. Enemy units within **Contagion Range** of this unit also have the effect of that **Plague** in addition to your army’s chosen **Plague** until the end of the battle.

## Stratagems

### TERRITORIAL INFECTION - 1CP

*Paragons of Putrescence Stratagem*

**WHEN:** Start of the Command phase.

**TARGET:** One friendly DEATH GUARD CHARACTER unit.

**EFFECT:** Your unit has +1 **OC** until the end of the turn.

### AGGRAVUS SPASMS - 1CP

*Paragons of Putrescence Stratagem*

**WHEN:** Start of your Shooting Phase.

**TARGET:** One friendly DEATH GUARD CHARACTER unit.

**EFFECT:** Select one **visible** enemy unit within **Contagion Range** of your unit. That enemy unit has +6" **detection range**.

### SIMULTANEOUS CONTAMINATION - 1CP

*Paragons of Putrescence Stratagem*

**WHEN:** Your Shooting phase, when a friendly DEATH GUARD CHARACTER unit **starts an action**.

**TARGET:** That DEATH GUARD CHARACTER unit.

**EFFECT:** That **action** does not prevent your unit from being **eligible to shoot**.
