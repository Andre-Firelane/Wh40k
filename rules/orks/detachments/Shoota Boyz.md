# Shoota Boyz

**Orks** - 1 DP detachment - Force Disposition: Purge the Foe

Source: <https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/orks/>

## Detachment rule

### Dakka! Dakka! Dakka!

Friendly ORKS INFANTRY units’ ranged attacks have:

- [Assault].
- While this unit is **riled up**, +3" **R**.

## Enhancements

### Supa-glowy Fing - 25 pts

ORKS INFANTRY model only (excluding WEIRDBOY models). This model’s ranged attacks have:

- [Anti-Infantry 4+].
- [Devastating Wounds].
- [SUPA-HAZARDOUS].

[SUPA-HAZARDOUS]: Each time a unit is **selected to shoot**, after that unit has resolved all of its attacks, make two **hazard rolls** for that unit for each [SUPA-HAZARDOUS] weapon you selected in the Select Weapons step.

### Targetin' Squigs - 20 pts

BIG MEK/BIGBOSS/WARBOSS model only. This unit’s ranged attacks (excluding [Torrent] attacks) have +3" **R**.

## Stratagems

### KUSTOM DAKKA - 1CP

*Shoota Boyz Stratagem*

**WHEN:** Your Shooting phase, when a friendly ORKS INFANTRY unit is **selected to shoot**.

**TARGET:** That ORKS INFANTRY unit.

**EFFECT:** Your unit’s ranged attacks that target a unit (excluding MONSTER/VEHICLE units) have +1 to **wound rolls**.

### GLOWIN' DAKKA - 1CP

*Shoota Boyz Stratagem*

**WHEN:** Your Shooting phase, when a friendly ORKS INFANTRY unit is **selected to shoot**.

**TARGET:** That ORKS INFANTRY unit.

**EFFECT:** Your unit’s ranged attacks that target a unit within 9" have +1 **AP**.

### NEVER ENOUGH DAKKA - 1CP

*Shoota Boyz Stratagem*

**WHEN:** Your Shooting phase, when a friendly ORKS INFANTRY unit is **selected to shoot**.

**TARGET:** That ORKS INFANTRY unit.

**EFFECT:** Your unit’s ranged attacks have [Sustained Hits 1].
